- 2026-11-22 · **[CLMM chapter](/docs/en/products/clmm):** Corrects seven of the eight CLMM pages against the program source, the published SDK and mainnet. No upstream PR has been opened. The changes are in this checkout.

**In short.** The CLMM pages described the program's overall structure largely accurately, but several behavior claims and some account-table details did not match the source. The fee-tier table had indexes 0–2 wrong. The math and fees pages said fees always come off the input, but nearly half the busiest pools take them from one fixed token. They said limit-order fills are fee-free and happen after the tick is crossed; both are wrong. Several pages said swaps and increase-liquidity create tick arrays, that a decrease closes a filled limit order, and that out-of-range positions can earn rewards. The code demos had one example that does not type-check against the SDK.

## Pages edited

- [Overview](/docs/en/products/clmm/overview): corrects the opening cost, the limit-order and OpenBook statements, and when fees are paid.
- [Accounts](/docs/en/products/clmm/accounts): corrects the fee-tier table, tick-array creation, the bitmap extension, NFT ownership and the lifecycle table.
- [Ticks and positions](/docs/en/products/clmm/ticks-and-positions): corrects tick-array creation and cost, NFT authority and metadata, and out-of-range rewards.
- [Math](/docs/en/products/clmm/math): corrects the step fee, limit-order matching and settlement, the dynamic-fee decay rule and the price-limit behavior.
- [Instructions](/docs/en/products/clmm/instructions): corrects `CreatePool` preconditions, `SwapRouterBaseIn`, reward and fee-collection rules, the limit-order effects and the state-change matrix.
- [Fees and rewards](/docs/en/products/clmm/fees): replaces the fee tiers and dynamic-fee ranges with live values, and corrects the fee side, reward settlement and collection.
- [Code demos](/docs/en/products/clmm/code-demos): fixes the increase-liquidity example, the pool-creation and limit-order notes, and the close-position warning.

The [CLMM landing page](/docs/en/products/clmm) checked out and is unchanged.

## The live fee tiers

## Which side pays the fee, and how much

The pages said every fee comes off the swap input, "same convention as CPMM". A pool's `fee_on` decides: `Token0Only` and `Token1Only` pools take the fee from the output in one direction. On 2026-11-22, 978 of the 2,000 highest-TVL pools were single-sided. Source: [pool_fee.rs L258–276](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-clmm/blob/6d7a0f82d2f293c553c69aaf943377367c6c4cc6/programs/amm/src/states/pool_fee.rs#L258-L276) · [swap_math.rs L133–160](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-clmm/blob/6d7a0f82d2f293c553c69aaf943377367c6c4cc6/programs/amm/src/libraries/swap_math.rs#L133-L160) · [swap.rs L235–292](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-clmm/blob/6d7a0f82d2f293c553c69aaf943377367c6c4cc6/programs/amm/src/instructions/swap.rs#L235-L292)

- **[Math, Fees on each step](/docs/en/products/clmm/math#fees-on-each-step):** the formula, the side, and where the LP share goes with no liquidity in range.
  - **Was:** "Trade fees are taken off the **input** amount in each step, same convention as CPMM: `step_fee_amount  = ceil(step_input * trade_fee_rate / 1_000_000)`"
  - **Now:** "if the step stops short of the next tick: fee = amount_remaining − amount_in; else: fee = ceil(amount_in * fee_rate / (1_000_000 − fee_rate))" … "fee on output … fee = ceil(gross_out * fee_rate / 1_000_000)" … "If no liquidity is in range when a fee is charged … the LP portion is added to the protocol counter instead". The worked example's fee step uses the same rule.
- **[Fees, Why fees are per-side](/docs/en/products/clmm/fees#why-fees-are-per-side) and [Per-swap fee split](/docs/en/products/clmm/fees#per-swap-fee-split):**
  - **Was:** "in CLMM the same rule applies at each step: **fees accrue in whichever token is the input for that step**." … "`step_lp` flows into `fee_growth_global_{input_side}_x64`"
  - **Now:** "**`Token0Only` / `Token1Only` pools** always charge the configured token, taking it from the input in one direction and from the output in the other." … "707 of the 2,000 highest-TVL CLMM pools were `Token0Only` and 271 `Token1Only`" … "`step_lp` flows into `fee_growth_global_{fee_side}_x64`"
- **[Math, Single-tick swap step](/docs/en/products/clmm/math#single-tick-swap-step):** a direction slip, and the Uniswap comparison.
  - **Was:** "For the opposite direction (`token1 → token0`, price going down)" … "The logic there matches Uniswap v3's `SwapMath.computeSwapStep` one-for-one."
  - **Now:** "(`token1 → token0`, price going up)" … "It follows Uniswap v3's `SwapMath.computeSwapStep` for input-side fees and adds an output-side fee mode for single-sided-fee pools."

## Limit orders

The math page said fills are fee-free and come after the tick is crossed. The program fills orders at the tick before crossing it, and charges the swapper the pool's fee on that portion, which LPs, protocol and fund share as usual. Source: [swap.rs L672–730](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-clmm/blob/6d7a0f82d2f293c553c69aaf943377367c6c4cc6/programs/amm/src/instructions/swap.rs#L672-L730) · [tick_array.rs L18–25](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-clmm/blob/6d7a0f82d2f293c553c69aaf943377367c6c4cc6/programs/amm/src/states/tick_array.rs#L18-L25) · [limit_order.rs L78–232](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-clmm/blob/6d7a0f82d2f293c553c69aaf943377367c6c4cc6/programs/amm/src/states/limit_order.rs#L78-L232) · [close_limit_order.rs L18–31](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-clmm/blob/6d7a0f82d2f293c553c69aaf943377367c6c4cc6/programs/amm/src/instructions/limit_order/close_limit_order.rs#L18-L31)

- **[Math, Interaction with the LP curve](/docs/en/products/clmm/math#interaction-with-the-lp-curve):**
  - **Was:** "1. Cross tick `t_cross` (apply LP `liquidity_net` change first…). 2. Fill any limit orders sitting at `t_cross`." … "the limit-order portion of the trade is fee-free for the swapper"
  - **Now:** "1. Move along the LP curve to the tick `t`. 2. Fill the limit orders sitting at `t`. … 3. Only then cross `t`" … "The fill is **not** fee-free: the swapper pays the pool's current fee rate … on the limit-order portion too (`amm_fee_amount`)."
- **[Math, Matching step](/docs/en/products/clmm/math#matching-step):** the settlement formula, rewritten from `settle_filled_order`.
  - **Was:** "filled_amount  = total_amount × (1 − tick_now.unfilled_ratio_x64 / order.unfilled_ratio_x64) if tick_now.order_phase > order.order_phase else 0"
  - **Now:** three cases on `order_phase`: not reached, partly filled from `settle_base` with a 1-unit dust deduction, and fully filled; payout is the output at the tick minus `settled_output`.
- **[Instructions, `DecreaseLimitOrder`](/docs/en/products/clmm/instructions#decreaselimitorder) and [Accounts, Lifecycle](/docs/en/products/clmm/accounts#lifecycle-quick-reference):** a decrease never closes the order.
  - **Was:** "If the new unfilled remainder is zero, the program closes the account and refunds rent to `owner`." … "| `DecreaseLimitOrder` | — | Closes `limitOrderState` if order is fully consumed |"
  - **Now:** "It never closes the account, even when nothing is left; call `CloseLimitOrder` for that."
- **[Accounts, `LimitOrderState`](/docs/en/products/clmm/accounts#limitorderstate):** what `filled_amount` gates.
  - **Was:** "`filled_amount: u64, // informational; computed precisely on settle`" … "**Close** — once `unfilled_amount == 0`, the account is closeable."
  - **Now:** "updated by settle and decrease; close needs it == total_amount" … "A filled order must be settled first, because only settle and decrease update `filled_amount`."
- **[Code demos, Increase / decrease an open order](/docs/en/products/clmm/code-demos#increase-/-decrease-an-open-order) and Common pitfalls:**
  - **Was:** "Both instructions revert with `InvalidOrderPhase` if the order has already been fully filled." … "**Calling `decreaseLimitOrder` on a fully-filled order** → `InvalidOrderPhase`."
  - **Now:** "On a fully filled order it just pays out the output and removes nothing. `increaseLimitOrder` reverts with `InvalidOrderPhase` as soon as the order's cohort has started filling." The example's tick check is now `<=`, because the program rejects an order at the current tick ([open_limit_order.rs L271–291](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-clmm/blob/6d7a0f82d2f293c553c69aaf943377367c6c4cc6/programs/amm/src/instructions/limit_order/open_limit_order.rs#L271-L291)).
