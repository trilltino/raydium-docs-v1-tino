## Open

- **11.22.26** · **[Issue #36](https://github.com/raydium-io/raydium-docs-v1/issues/36), compute-unit figures:** Opened 22 November and still open. Reports that the compute figures for AMM v4, CPMM, CLMM, routing and Stable AMM don't match mainnet. The priority-fee and transaction tables are 3–9× too high, and the Stable AMM figure at least 6× too low. Five statements are wrong, including that CPMM is cheaper than AMM v4 and that a Compute Budget instruction costs 5,000 CU. Each figure was measured on 9 September and on 22 November with `scripts/measure-compute.py`; the evidence is in `public/tino-changes/evidence/`.
