<Info>
  Raydium trade fees are split between liquidity providers, RAY buybacks, and treasury destinations. CPMM can charge a separate creator fee. Split percentages apply to the relevant fee or calculated PnL growth, not automatically to the full trade amount.
</Info>

## Fee types

Raydium has five user-facing fee categories.

| Fee type | When it applies | What it pays for |
|---|---|---|
| Trade fee | Every AMM swap | LP earnings, RAY buybacks, and treasury share |
| CPMM creator fee | CPMM swaps when enabled with a nonzero config rate | Recorded pool creator, less the protocol share at collection |
| Pool creation fee | When creating CPMM or Standard AMM v4 pools | Spam prevention and protocol infrastructure |
| Rent | When creating pools, positions, farms, or token accounts | Solana account storage |
| LaunchLab fees | During token launches and graduation | LaunchLab creator and protocol fee flow |

Rent is paid to create Solana accounts. It is separate from Raydium protocol fees.

## Trade-fee split

| Pool type | LP share | RAY buyback | Treasury |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CLMM | 84% | 12% | 4% |
| CPMM | 84% | 12% | 4% |
| Standard AMM v4 | ≈88% economic equivalent | ≈12% economic equivalent | — |

For CPMM and CLMM, these percentages apply to the pool's **trade fee**. Example with a `0.25%` trade fee:

```text
Trade fee:       0.25% of trade
LP share:        0.25% * 84% = 0.2100% of trade
RAY buyback:     0.25% * 12% = 0.0300% of trade
Treasury share:  0.25% * 4%  = 0.0100% of trade
```

AMM v4 does not apply 12% directly to each swap fee. Its default `pnl_numerator / pnl_denominator = 12 / 100` applies to pool growth calculated by `calc_take_pnl`. The approximately 0.22% LP and 0.03% protocol figures are economic equivalents under the defaults, subject to settlement timing and integer rounding. See [`products/amm-v4/fees`](/docs/en/products/amm-v4/fees).

## Separate CPMM creator fee

CPMM's creator fee uses `AmmConfig.creator_fee_rate` independently of the trade fee. It does not reduce the 84/12/4 trade-fee split. The default creator rate is zero.

When the path is enabled and the rate is nonzero, fees accrue to `PoolState.pool_creator`. Normal collection requires that creator to sign. Permissionless collection can be triggered by another payer but cannot redirect the creator's share. At collection, the configured protocol share enters protocol accounting and the creator receives the remainder. See [`products/cpmm/fees`](/docs/en/products/cpmm/fees).

## Swap-fee tiers

| Product | Common fee tiers | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| CLMM | 0.01%, 0.05%, 0.25%, 1% | Fee tier is tied to the pool's AmmConfig. |
| CPMM | 0.01%, 0.25%, 1% | Default user-facing tier is commonly 0.25%. |
| Standard AMM v4 | 0.25% | Legacy constant-product pools. |
| LaunchLab pre-graduation | 1% buy and sell fee | Applies while the token trades on the bonding curve. |

## Pool creation fees

Creating a Standard AMM v4 or CPMM pool costs `0.15 SOL` in pool creation fees.

| Pool type | Pool creation fee |
|---|---:|
| CPMM | 0.15 SOL |
| Standard AMM v4 | 0.15 SOL |
| CLMM | No separate Raydium pool creation fee; rent still applies |

Pool creation fees are separate from account rent and transaction fees.

## Fee denominator conventions

Different Raydium programs encode fee rates with different denominators.

| Product | Denominator | Example |
|---|---:|---|
| AMM v4 | 10,000 | `25` means `0.25%` |
| CPMM | 1,000,000 | `2,500` means `0.25%` |
| CLMM | 1,000,000 | `2,500` means `0.25%` |
| LaunchLab | 1,000,000 or program-specific config fields | Check LaunchConfig fields before integrating |

This matters when reading raw on-chain accounts or SDK responses. Always normalize rates before comparing products.

## How fees accrue

1. A trader pays the trade fee and any enabled CPMM creator fee as part of the swap.
2. The LP share remains in or accrues to the pool's accounting.
3. Buyback, treasury, and creator shares accumulate in their respective program fee buckets.
4. Collection transactions move those balances to the relevant addresses listed in [`ray/treasury`](/docs/en/ray/treasury). CPMM creator collection first applies the protocol's share of the creator bucket.
5. AMM v4 is the exception to per-swap protocol booking: it realizes protocol PnL later from calculated pool growth.

For LPs, fees are realized differently by product:

- **AMM v4 and CPMM:** LP fees accumulate in pool reserves and are realized when LP tokens are burned on withdrawal.
- **CLMM:** LP fees accrue per position and can be claimed with the fee-collection flow.
- **LaunchLab:** launch-specific creator and protocol fees follow LaunchLab configuration.

## Token-2022 transfer fees

Token-2022 transfer fees are not Raydium fees.

If a Token-2022 mint has a transfer fee, that fee is paid to the mint's configured authority. A swap involving Token-2022 tokens may therefore include:

```text
Raydium pool fee + Token-2022 transfer fee
```

The two fees have different destinations and should be displayed separately in integrations.

## Practical pitfalls

- **Do not multiply by the wrong base.** For CPMM and CLMM, a 12% buyback share is 12% of the trade fee, not 12% of the trade. AMM v4 applies its default 12% PnL fraction to calculated growth instead.
- **Do not combine CPMM fee buckets.** The creator fee has an independent rate; the protocol's share of it is applied at collection.
- **Normalize denominators.** AMM v4 uses a different denominator than CPMM and CLMM.
- **Separate protocol fees from network fees.** Solana base fees and priority fees are paid to the network, not Raydium.
- **Account for Token-2022 fees.** Transfer fees can materially change the user's effective received amount.
- **Fetch fresh config.** If your integration caches AmmConfig data, refresh before building high-value transactions.
