<Info>
  Creator fees have two separate phases. Locked-LP Fee Keys are a third mechanism. Before the 2026-08-17 upgrade, the creator scale produced a creator Fee Key. Migrations executed after the upgrade assign that locked-LP share to the platform.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Fee settings depend on the platform and CPMM config. Verify the on-chain values before signing. This page explains mechanics only and is not financial, legal, tax, or investment advice.
</Warning>

## Phase 1: bonding-curve creator fees

While your token trades on the bonding curve, the platform can charge a creator-fee component on buys and sells.

- The fee accrues in the quote token, usually SOL.
- `PlatformConfig.creator_fee_rate` sets the rate.
- The creator-fee vault is keyed by **creator and quote mint**. The same creator can reuse that vault across launches sharing the quote mint; it is not one vault per token.
- The creator claims an available balance through LaunchLab `ClaimCreatorFee`, including through the supported Portfolio flow.
- Migration stops new bonding-curve accrual. An existing vault balance remains separate from CPMM fees.

## Phase 2: CPMM creator fees

Every newly initialized launch uses the CPMM migration path. A nonzero `AmmConfig.creator_fee_rate` alone is not enough to make creator fees accrue. Check all three settings:

| Setting | What it controls |
|---|---|
| `AmmConfig.creator_fee_rate` | The independent creator-fee rate |
| `AmmConfig.enable_creator_fee` | Whether the selected config enables the creator-fee path |
| `PoolState.creator_fee_on` | Which token side bears the creator fee |

By default, the launch creator becomes the CPMM pool creator. A platform can instead set `platformCpCreator`, in which case that address receives the CPMM creator-fee rights.

The recorded CPMM creator can use creator-signed collection. Another payer can trigger permissionless collection, but CPMM fixes the creator's destinations to its canonical associated token accounts.

At collection, CPMM applies the configured `creator_fee_share_rate` or a per-creator override. The protocol retains that share of the accrued creator-fee bucket, and the remainder goes to the recorded creator. Gross accrued creator fees are therefore not necessarily the creator's net payout.

## What happens to LP tokens

The platform config stores `platformScale`, `creatorScale`, and `burnScale`. Before the 2026-08-17 upgrade, `creatorScale` created a creator-owned Fee Key. For migrations executed after the upgrade, LaunchLab combines it with `platformScale`:

```text
platform locked LP = platformScale + creatorScale
burned LP          = burnScale
```

If the locked share is nonzero, one Fee Key NFT goes to the platform's configured lock-NFT wallet. This NFT controls LP-fee harvesting. It does not control CPMM creator-fee collection. Creator Fee Keys from migrations completed before the upgrade remain with their existing holders.

## Claim paths

| Revenue | Beneficiary | Claim path |
|---|---|---|
| Bonding-curve creator fee | Original launch creator | LaunchLab `ClaimCreatorFee` from the creator/quote-mint vault |
| CPMM creator fee | Recorded CPMM `pool_creator`, less the protocol share at collection | Creator-signed collection or permissionless collection to canonical ATAs |
| Locked-LP fees from a historical creator Fee Key | Current holder of that Fee Key | LP-Lock / Burn & Earn harvest |
| Locked-LP fees from an updated migration | Current holder of the platform Fee Key | LP-Lock / Burn & Earn harvest |

## Fee flow summary

```text
Bonding curve
  creator fee → vault keyed by (launch creator, quote mint)
              → ClaimCreatorFee

Migration to CPMM
  Before 2026-08-17 upgrade → platform Fee Key + creator Fee Key + burned share
  After upgrade             → combined platform Fee Key share + burned share

CPMM trading
  creator fee → accrued creator bucket
              → protocol share at collection
              → remainder to recorded pool creator

Locked LP
  LP fee → current Fee Key holder
```

## Before you launch

- Check the bonding-curve `creator_fee_rate` and the creator/quote-mint vault path.
- Check whether `platformCpCreator` replaces you as the CPMM creator-of-record.
- Check the CPMM config's enablement, creator-fee rate, fee-on setting, and creator-fee share rate or override.
- For migrations executed after the upgrade, do not treat `creatorScale` as a promised creator Fee Key allocation. For earlier migrations, verify the existing Fee Key record.
- Keep referrals separate: `shareFeeRate` pays a trade referrer's quote-token ATA and does not accrue in the creator vault.

## Verification boundary

CPMM creator-fee behavior above follows the public CPMM program. LaunchLab and LP-Lock implementation source is not publicly available; their claim paths and migration ownership model are documented from the published IDL, SDK, configuration, and migration references.
