<Info>
  **What "burn" means here.** Despite the name, the selected CPMM LP amount or CLMM position NFT is **locked** in program-owned escrow, not destroyed. That liquidity cannot be withdrawn, but its LP fees continue to accrue. The transferable Fee Key NFT represents the right to collect them.
</Info>

<Warning>
  A frozen CLMM position from a restricted-issuer pool cannot enter Burn & Earn because its position NFT cannot be transferred into the lock escrow. Check the position NFT token account before offering this flow. Ordinary CLMM positions remain transferable and supported.
</Warning>

## What Burn & Earn is

Burn & Earn lets a pool creator or another LP make selected liquidity **permanent** on-chain while keeping a separate right to harvest its LP trading fees. CPMM supports locking all or part of an LP balance. CLMM locks a whole position NFT.

Common uses include committing project liquidity, transferring LP-fee rights without transferring the underlying liquidity, and separating treasury liquidity custody from fee collection.

## Supported pool types

| Program | Burn & Earn support |
|---|---|
| CPMM | Full or partial LP-balance lock |
| CLMM | Whole position NFT; full-range positions are recommended |
| AMM v4 | Not supported |

For CLMM, consider the permanence of the selected range. Once locked, you cannot rebalance. If price moves out of that range, the position stops accruing new fees and rewards until price returns. A full-range position avoids that range-exit risk, but does not remove market or smart-contract risk.

## How the lock works at the program level

1. The selected CPMM LP amount or whole CLMM position NFT moves into program-owned escrow.
2. LP-Lock mints a **Fee Key NFT** to the configured `fee_nft_owner`. In the Raydium UI this is normally your connected wallet. Metadata is optional and depends on the lock call's `with_metadata` argument.
3. The current Fee Key holder can collect the locked position's LP fees:
   - CPMM uses `collect_cp_fees`.
   - CLMM uses `collect_clmm_fees_and_rewards`.
4. The underlying LP amount or position never leaves escrow through a withdrawal path.

The lock instructions are `lock_cp_liquidity` and `lock_clmm_position`. The official CPI interface accepts an explicit CPMM `lp_amount`, which is why a CPMM lock does not have to consume the full balance.

```text
CPMM wallet LP ── selected amount ──→ LP-Lock escrow
CLMM position NFT ── whole position ─→ LP-Lock escrow
                                      │
                                      └─→ Fee Key NFT → current holder collects LP fees
```

The Fee Key is a standard SPL NFT and can be transferred between wallets. Marketplace, collateral, and other contract support depend on those third parties; they are not guarantees made by LP-Lock. Whoever holds the Fee Key when the applicable collection instruction executes receives the locked position's LP fees.

A CPMM creator fee is a separate entitlement. It goes to `PoolState.pool_creator`, less the protocol share applied at collection. Locking an LP position or transferring its Fee Key does not transfer the `pool_creator` designation or its creator-fee stream. See [`products/cpmm/fees`](/docs/en/products/cpmm/fees).

## UI walk-through

1. Open the [CPMM lock page](https://raydium.io/liquidity/lock) or [CLMM lock page](https://raydium.io/clmm/lock).
2. Select the pool or position to lock.
3. For CPMM, choose the exact amount or a percentage offered by the connected UI. Available controls can depend on the position. The program supports a partial lock through its `lp_amount` argument. For CLMM, review the whole position and its range.
4. Verify the pool, position, amount, and irreversible lock. Type the confirmation sentence the UI shows.
5. Click **Confirm, lock the liquidity permanently** and approve the transaction.
6. Confirm that the Fee Key NFT arrived in your wallet and that the Portfolio page shows the locked position.

Claim through the Portfolio page. Integrators use `collect_cp_fees` for CPMM or `collect_clmm_fees_and_rewards` for CLMM rather than a generic `collectFees` instruction.

## Verifying a lock

Verify the actual locked amount and the pool's LP supply or position record on-chain. An API or UI percentage can help locate a lock, but it does not establish the safety of the whole token.

A high locked share in one pool does not establish that the token is safe. Check:

- liquidity in other pools;
- unlocked LP held elsewhere;
- token-holder concentration;
- mint or Token-2022 authorities and extensions;
- the security of the token or its project.

Do not describe a token as protected from every liquidity withdrawal solely because one pool reports a high lock percentage.

## Pitfalls

- **Irreversible.** The locked LP side cannot be retrieved. Confirm the exact amount before signing.
- **CLMM range risk is permanent until price returns.** A locked out-of-range position cannot be rebalanced into a new range.
- **Fee Key ownership controls collection.** Transferring it transfers future LP-fee rights. Losing or burning it can make those rights inaccessible.
- **Token-2022 deductions are separate.** A transfer-fee mint can reduce the net amount received during transfers and fee claims.
- **CPMM permits partial locks.** You do not need to split a CPMM position merely to lock 50% of its LP balance. CLMM still locks the whole position NFT.
