<Info>
  A LaunchLab platform is an on-chain configuration used by a launch surface or community. Platform settings can affect fees, branding, creator-fee support, launch restrictions, and post-migration pool behavior.
</Info>

LaunchLab supports third-party launch environments through **PlatformConfig PDAs**. A platform can offer its own branded token-launch experience while using LaunchLab program infrastructure and Raydium's authorized CPMM migration path.

## What a platform can control

| Configuration | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| `creatorFeeRate` | Sets the bonding-curve creator-fee component. It is separate from CPMM creator fees. |
| `feeRate` and `platformClaimFeeWallet` | Sets the platform trade-fee component and the wallet authorized to claim it. Pre-migration platform fees accrue to a claim vault; they are not simply sent to that wallet on each trade. |
| `name`, `web`, `img` | Stores the platform's on-chain branding strings. There are no `description` or `logo` fields in the SDK layout. |
| `cpConfigId` | Selects the CPMM `AmmConfig` used for migration. |
| `platformCpCreator` | Optionally replaces the launch creator as the post-migration CPMM creator-of-record. |
| `platformScale`, `creatorScale`, `burnScale` | Determines locked and burned LP. Under the updated migration flow, the first two combine into the platform-owned locked share. |
| `platformLockNftWallet` | Receives the platform Fee Key when the locked share is nonzero. |
| `platformVestingWallet`, `platformVestingScale` | Controls the platform's vesting beneficiary and share. |
| `transferFeeExtensionAuth` | Controls applicable Token-2022 transfer-fee authorities during launch and migration. |
| `restrictGlobalConfig` | Can require an allow record for the selected `GlobalConfig`. |
| `restrictCurveParam`, `curveRuleManager` | Enables `PlatformCurveRule` checks and delegates rule management without transferring platform-admin ownership. |

PlatformConfig does not give the platform arbitrary curve math or permission to migrate launches itself. `GlobalConfig` still supplies protocol limits and the authorized migration wallet.

## Three different post-launch rights

Do not combine these revenue paths:

1. **Pre-migration platform fees** accrue to platform fee vaults and are claimed by `platformClaimFeeWallet`.
2. **Locked-LP fees** belong to the current holder of the platform Fee Key sent to `platformLockNftWallet`.
3. **CPMM creator fees** follow the recorded CPMM `pool_creator`, which can be the launch creator or `platformCpCreator`, subject to CPMM enablement, rates, and collection rules.

```text
PlatformConfig
  ├─ feeRate → platform fee vault → platformClaimFeeWallet
  ├─ LP lock scales → LP-Lock → Fee Key at platformLockNftWallet
  ├─ platformCpCreator → CPMM pool_creator → CPMM creator-fee rights
  ├─ vesting wallet/scale → separate platform vesting record
  └─ launch restrictions → allowed GlobalConfig + PlatformCurveRule
```

## What creators should check

Before launching under any platform, confirm:

- The platform fee rate and claim wallet.
- The bonding-curve creator fee rate.
- The CPMM config, creator-fee settings, and creator-of-record.
- The LP lock and burn scales and the platform Fee Key recipient.
- Any platform vesting share and beneficiary.
- Token-2022 transfer-fee authorities where applicable.
- Global-config allowlist and curve-rule restrictions.
- The platform's own terms, policies, or eligibility rules.

<Warning>
  Platform settings can materially change creator economics and user expectations. Review them before signing the launch transaction. Mutable platform settings can also differ between launch creation and migration; read the relevant values at both points.
</Warning>

## What traders should check

When buying a LaunchLab token, the platform can affect the fee path and post-migration liquidity handling. Before trading:

- Check the token's platform, quote mint, curve type, and launch state.
- Check the protocol, platform, creator, and any referral fee components.
- Check whether LP is configured to be burned or locked, then verify the actual migration records after migration.
- Verify the token mint from the token page and an independent source where possible.
