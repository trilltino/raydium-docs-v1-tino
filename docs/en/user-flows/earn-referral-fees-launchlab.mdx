<Info>
  LaunchLab referrals are separate from [Raydium Swap referrals and Blinks](/docs/en/user-flows/referrals-and-blinks). On 2026-11-19, the Raydium LaunchLab UI was recorded using a **1%** referral rate. Integrators can choose a different per-trade rate within the selected `GlobalConfig` cap.
</Info>

<Warning>
  Referral links do not verify token quality or safety. Only share tokens you are willing to stand behind, and make sure your audience can verify the mint address and launch settings themselves.
</Warning>

## How it works

Any wallet holder can act as a referrer; you do not need to be the token's creator. When a trade carries your wallet as its referral recipient, the referral component is paid in the launch's quote token as part of that confirmed trade transaction.

For the Raydium LaunchLab UI observed on 2026-11-19:

- The referral rate is **1%** of the applicable trade amount.
- Buys and sells on the bonding curve can carry the referral.
- Payment goes to your quote-token associated token account, not to a pending off-chain balance.
- There is no separate LaunchLab referral claim step.

"Real time" means settlement in the trader's confirmed transaction. It does not mean a separate payment service or an off-chain rewards balance.

## Link and attribution scope

The Raydium UI uses the `lreferrer` URL/query parameter to carry the referrer address. If a recipient navigates to another token and that parameter survives in the trade URL or application state, the later trade can remain attributed to you. If the parameter is removed or replaced, do not assume the referral still applies.

This is conditional URL propagation, not a promise of a durable browser-session referral store.

## Generating your referral link

1. Connect your wallet on [raydium.io/launchpad](https://raydium.io/launchpad).
2. Navigate to any LaunchLab token's detail page.
3. Click **Share** on the token page.
4. Copy the generated link and verify that it contains your wallet address in `lreferrer`.
5. Share the link with your audience.

<Note>
  Connect the wallet that should receive the fees before creating the link. Anyone inspecting the link can identify the referral address.
</Note>

## What you earn

| Scenario | Raydium UI observed on 2026-11-19 |
|---|---|
| Attributed buy of the linked token | 1% of the applicable trade amount |
| Attributed sell of the linked token | 1% of the applicable trade amount |
| Attributed trade of another LaunchLab token | 1% only if your `lreferrer` value remains attached to that trade |
| Trade without your referral parameter | No referral payment to you |

The program accepts a per-trade `shareFeeRate`. It must not exceed `GlobalConfig.maxShareFeeRate`; the live public configuration's cap was 1% when checked on 2026-11-19. Raydium's 1% UI rate is therefore a product choice, not a universal rate for every integration.

## Fee accounting

Referral fees are one component of the curve trade's total charges:

```text
protocol_fee = protocol rate × trade amount
platform_fee = platform rate × trade amount
creator_fee  = creator rate × trade amount
referral_fee = shareFeeRate × trade amount
```

Rates use integer base units and their configured denominators. Program rounding can create small differences from a decimal estimate. Review the trade quote and actual balance changes rather than assuming the referral is the only fee.

```text
Shared URL with lreferrer
          ↓
Recipient opens token and keeps the referral parameter
          ↓
Trade transaction includes shareFeeRate + referral recipient
          ↓
Confirmed trade pays the referrer's quote-token ATA
```

For the SDK parameters and remaining accounts, see [LaunchLab code demos](/docs/en/products/launchlab/code-demos) and [LaunchLab instructions](/docs/en/products/launchlab/instructions).
