<Warning>
  LaunchLab is permissionless token-launch infrastructure. Anyone can create a token, choose a name or ticker, and configure permitted launch parameters. Always verify mint addresses and launch settings before buying or promoting a token. This page explains product mechanics only and is not financial, legal, or investment advice.
</Warning>

LaunchLab lets creators issue tokens and bootstrap initial liquidity through a bonding curve. Buyers trade against the curve during fundraising. Reaching the funding threshold makes the launch ready to migrate, but does not itself create the CPMM pool. The migration wallet configured on `GlobalConfig` must then execute `MigrateToCpswap` before normal CPMM trading begins.

## Who uses LaunchLab

| Audience | Common tasks |
|---|---|
| Traders | Discover early-stage tokens, buy or sell while the launch is in `Fund`, and track migration progress. |
| Creators | Launch a token without code, choose parameters allowed by the selected configs, and potentially earn creator fees. |
| Platforms | Build branded launch environments with platform-specific fee settings, branding, launch rules, and migration settings. |

## How a launch works

1. A creator configures token metadata and launch parameters. `GlobalConfig` supplies protocol and deployment limits; `PlatformConfig` supplies platform fees, launch rules, and migration settings.
2. The launch opens in `Fund`. Buyers and sellers trade directly against the selected bonding curve.
3. Price behavior depends on the curve. Constant-product and linear curves change price with trading; a fixed-price curve keeps its configured price constant.
4. When the launch meets its funding condition, its status becomes `Migrate`. Curve trading is no longer available in this intermediate state.
5. The authorized `migrate_to_cpswap_wallet` executes migration. LaunchLab creates the CPMM pool, handles LP according to the platform configuration, and moves the launch to `Trade`.
6. In `Trade`, users trade the token through Raydium Swap and aggregators that have indexed the resulting pool.

```text
Initialize → Fund ⇄ Buy / Sell
                 │
                 │ funding condition met
                 ▼
              Migrate
                 │
                 │ authorized MigrateToCpswap transaction
                 ▼
        CPMM pool created → Trade
```

There is no permissionless migration caller bounty in this flow. Reaching the threshold and completing migration are separate events.

## Creation modes

| Mode | Use it when | Key settings |
|---|---|---|
| JustSendIt | You want the Raydium UI's streamlined launch with deployment defaults. | Name, ticker, image, optional social links, optional first buy. |
| Advanced | You need to choose from the supply presets and configure the permitted raise target, curve allocation, or vesting. | Supply preset, raise target, curve allocation, vesting, and platform-defined migration settings. |

JustSendIt uses the selected deployment's default funding target. Advanced exposes more launch parameters, subject to UI restrictions, `GlobalConfig` limits, and any platform curve rules. Neither mode lets a creator independently override all global or platform fee settings.

## Graduation and liquidity

Graduation is a two-stage process: the launch first enters `Migrate`, then the authorized wallet creates the CPMM pool. LP treatment and the CPMM creator-of-record depend on platform configuration.

- All newly initialized launches use the CPMM migration path.
- The platform and creator scale fields combine into one platform-owned locked-LP share for migrations under the updated program; the remaining configured share is burned.
- CPMM creator fees are separate from the platform Fee Key. They accrue only when the selected CPMM configuration and pool settings enable them, and they follow the recorded CPMM pool creator.

## Account relationships

```text
GlobalConfig
  ├─ launch limits and protocol fee rate
  └─ authorized CPMM migration wallet

PlatformConfig
  ├─ platform and bonding-curve creator fee rates
  ├─ branding and launch restrictions
  ├─ LP lock/burn scales and Fee Key recipient
  └─ CPMM config and optional pool-creator override
          │
Creator ──→ Launch PoolState ──→ CPMM PoolState
          │        │                  └─ creator-fee recipient
          │        └─ creator/quote-mint fee vault
          └─ separate vesting records per beneficiary
```

## Safety basics

- Verify the token mint and quote mint, not only the ticker or logo.
- Review the funding target, curve type, curve progress, and launch status before trading.
- Check protocol, platform, creator, and any referral fees before launching or buying.
- For Token-2022 launches, inspect transfer-fee settings and authorities.
- Treat platform Fee Key NFTs as LP-fee-rights assets. Transferring one transfers its future fee rights; burning or losing it can make those rights inaccessible.
- Read [Trust and safety](/docs/en/getting-started/trust-and-safety) before connecting a wallet or signing transactions.
