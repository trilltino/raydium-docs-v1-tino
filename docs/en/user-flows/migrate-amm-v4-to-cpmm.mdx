<Info>
  AMM v4 is fully operational and has no announced sunset. Migration is a choice, not a forced move. CPMM supports Token-2022 and configurable fee accounts. Neither current AMM v4 nor CPMM depends on OpenBook.
</Info>

## Why migrate

| Dimension | AMM v4 | CPMM |
|-----------|--------|------|
| Default LP economic share of volume | 0.22% under default parameters | 0.21% under the standard 0.25% config with an 84% LP trade-fee share |
| Fee configuration | Per-pool values; the admin can change them with `SetParams` | Selected `AmmConfig`; a separate creator fee can also apply |
| OpenBook dependency | Removed in July 2026 | None |
| Token-2022 support | No | Supported within program constraints |
| Position representation | Fungible LP tokens | Fungible LP tokens |

Compare the live source and destination configurations rather than assuming migration improves LP fees. Also account for changes in liquidity depth, price exposure, farm rewards, and coordination costs.

## Pre-migration checklist

For pool operators and project treasuries:

- [ ] Announce the migration timeline to LPs.
- [ ] Give LPs at least 48 hours where possible.
- [ ] Confirm the destination CPMM pool identity and mints.
- [ ] Read the destination `AmmConfig`, including its trade-fee split and creator-fee settings.
- [ ] Prepare post-migration announcements with the new pool ID and LP mint.
- [ ] Decide whether a new farm is needed and when old and new reward streams change.

For individual LPs:

- [ ] Confirm you are following the project's endorsed migration.
- [ ] Keep enough SOL for multiple transactions and token-account rent.
- [ ] Note your AMM v4 LP balance and expected underlying amounts.
- [ ] Check whether your AMM v4 LP tokens are staked in a farm.
- [ ] Verify the destination pool's mints, fee config, and liquidity before depositing.

## UI migration: individual LP

Migration has two main **liquidity operations**: remove liquidity from AMM v4, then add liquidity to CPMM. They are not one atomic migration instruction and do not necessarily equal two transactions. Unstaking, destination-pool creation, ATA creation, and new-farm staking can add transactions.

```text
Optional: unstake AMM v4 LP and settle farm rewards
                    ↓
        Withdraw AMM v4 LP → receive both tokens
                    ↓
   Select or create the intended CPMM pool and required ATAs
                    ↓
          Deposit tokens → receive CPMM LP tokens
                    ↓
        Optional: stake CPMM LP in the destination farm
```

### Step 1: withdraw from AMM v4

1. Open the AMM v4 pool or your portfolio position.
2. If your LP tokens are staked in a farm, unstake them first.
3. Click **Remove liquidity**.
4. Choose the percentage to withdraw.
5. Review the token amounts you will receive.
6. Confirm and approve.

### Step 2: deposit to CPMM

1. Open the destination CPMM pool.
2. Click **Add liquidity**.
3. Enter the amount of one token.
4. Review the paired token amount and any leftover balance.
5. Confirm and approve.

Between withdrawal and deposit, pool prices can move. Different pool ratios can leave a meaningful amount of one token in your wallet; it is not necessarily rounding dust.

Withdrawing and re-depositing also changes your position's exposure to price divergence and fee accrual. Compare the value and token amounts at each step rather than treating the new LP balance as the same position. If you unstake or change farms, check when each reward stream stops and starts accruing.

## UI migration: project treasury

For a project treasury, the process is the same but requires coordination:

1. **Announce** the migration ahead of time.
2. **Seed the CPMM pool** before asking users to migrate.
3. **Migrate treasury LP** first and publish transaction links.
4. **Create a new farm** if incentives should continue on the CPMM LP mint.
5. **Monitor individual LP migrations** over the next days or weeks.
6. **Delist or stop promoting the old pool** only after users have had enough time to move. The project cannot disable a permissionless pool for everyone.

## Verification

### Post-withdraw

- AMM v4 LP token balance should decrease or reach zero.
- The two underlying tokens should appear in your wallet.
- The transaction should show **Success** on a Solana explorer.

### Post-deposit

- CPMM LP token balance should appear in your wallet.
- Raydium's portfolio should show the new position.
- The CPMM pool page should show your liquidity share.

### Proof of migration

For transparency, publish the relevant transaction links, including unstaking or pool-creation transactions where applicable:

- Withdraw from AMM v4.
- Deposit to CPMM.

## Rollback considerations

Rolling back from CPMM to AMM v4 means withdrawing from CPMM and depositing to AMM v4 through another non-atomic sequence. Current AMM v4 pool creation does not require OpenBook market setup. Compare current creation costs, fee settings, liquidity, and farm support before changing pools again.

## Common concerns

### My AMM v4 LP is staked in a farm. Can I migrate?

Yes. Unstake first, withdraw LP from AMM v4, deposit into CPMM, then stake the new CPMM LP tokens if a new farm exists. Review pending-reward settlement at unstaking and the new farm's reward schedule.

### The CPMM pool does not exist yet

Most LPs should wait for the project to identify a destination pool. Creating one yourself adds pool-creation steps and makes you responsible for its initial price, liquidity, and configuration.

### I have a large position. What about slippage?

Review each withdrawal and deposit quote. Splitting execution into smaller slices does not automatically reduce total price impact and adds transaction costs. Compare the complete expected amounts and costs before signing.

### What happens to LP tokens I forget to migrate?

They stay in the old pool and keep earning according to AMM v4 rules. Nothing forces migration.

## Troubleshooting

### Destination LP token account is new

The first CPMM deposit may need to create a new LP token account. Keep extra SOL in your wallet for rent and fees.

### A later transaction expires

If withdrawal lands but deposit expires, your tokens remain in your wallet. Reopen the verified CPMM pool, refresh the quote, and retry. The migration is not atomic.

### Wrong CPMM pool

Multiple CPMM pools can exist for the same pair. Verify the pool address, mints, `AmmConfig`, creator-fee settings, and liquidity rather than relying on the token symbols alone.
