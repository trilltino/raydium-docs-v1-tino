<Info>
  Rewards accrue automatically, but they remain pending until you claim them. This page covers **farm harvest** for staked positions and **CLMM collection** for concentrated-liquidity positions.
</Info>

## Claim paths at a glance

| Position or revenue | Underlying claim path |
|---|---|
| CPMM LP fees | Fees remain in pool reserves; withdraw LP to realize your share |
| CLMM LP fees and position rewards | `DecreaseLiquidity` or `DecreaseLiquidityV2`, including zero-liquidity collection |
| Farm v6 and legacy Farm v3/v5 rewards | `Withdraw` with `amount = 0` |
| Locked-position LP fees | LP-Lock collection by the current Fee Key holder; see [Burn & Earn](/docs/en/user-flows/burn-and-earn) |

```text
CPMM fee → pool reserves → LP withdrawal
CLMM fee/reward → position counters → DecreaseLiquidity
Farm reward → farm ledger → Withdraw(0) on Farm v3, v5 or v6
```

## Farm harvest

Farm rewards accrue while your eligible LP tokens are staked in an active farm. Harvesting transfers pending rewards to your wallet. It does not withdraw your stake.

### UI walkthrough

1. Open [raydium.io/portfolio](https://raydium.io/portfolio).
2. Find your farm position.
3. Click **Harvest**.
4. Review pending amounts per reward stream.
5. Confirm and approve in your wallet.

**Harvest** is the UI action. On Farm v6 and on legacy Farm v3 and v5, the SDK behind it sends `Withdraw` with `amount = 0`, which settles rewards without changing your stake. Identify the farm's program version before building or debugging a transaction.

## Combined deposit or withdraw with harvest

Farm deposits and withdrawals can settle pending rewards as part of the same action. If you add or remove stake, review the confirmation carefully because it may also harvest pending rewards.

## CLMM collect fees

Only in-range CLMM liquidity accrues new fees. Each swap accrues its fee in the token entering the pool, so both pool mints can accrue over multiple trade directions. Fees remain pending on the position and do not auto-compound.

1. Open your CLMM position from [raydium.io/portfolio](https://raydium.io/portfolio).
2. Click **Collect fees**.
3. Review the token amounts.
4. Confirm and approve.

To compound manually, collect fees, rebalance the two tokens if needed, then increase liquidity on the position.

## CLMM collect rewards

Some CLMM pools have reward streams in addition to swap fees. Only in-range liquidity accrues new position rewards.

1. Open the CLMM position.
2. Click **Collect rewards**.
3. Review reward mints and amounts.
4. Confirm and approve.

If the UI offers **Collect all**, use it to collect fees and rewards together.

### On-chain collection mechanism

The UI's **Collect fees** and **Collect rewards** labels do not name separate program instructions. CLMM pays both through `DecreaseLiquidity` or `DecreaseLiquidityV2`. To collect without reducing liquidity, use:

```text
liquidity    = 0
amount_0_min = 0
amount_1_min = 0
```

Each active reward stream also needs a remaining-account group in this order:

```text
reward_token_vault (writable)
recipient_token_account (writable)
reward_vault_mint
```

The program pays pending fees and rewards and leaves position liquidity unchanged. `CollectRemainingRewards` is different: it lets a reward funder recover undistributed budget after the stream ends. See [CLMM reward collection](/docs/en/products/clmm/instructions#collecting-rewards).

## Claim costs

Every claim costs a Solana transaction fee and may include a priority fee during congestion. Compare the pending amount with that cost and whether you intend to compound it. No protocol rule requires a particular claim cadence.

## Troubleshooting

### Harvesting after a reward ends

Ending a stream stops new emissions. Claiming previously earned rewards still requires available reward-vault funds and a farm state that has not been wound down. Do not assume an ended display label alone determines whether a claim can succeed.

### Transfer-fee reward mints

If a reward mint charges transfer fees, your net received amount can be lower than the pending display. Review wallet balance changes after confirmation.

### A harvest or collection fails

Check the specific path rather than only refreshing the page:

- Confirm the farm is v6 or legacy v3/v5 and use the matching claim instruction.
- Confirm all required recipient ATAs and reward remaining accounts are present.
- Refresh recently created, migrated, or changed position data before rebuilding the transaction.
- Confirm the pending amount is nonzero.
- Keep enough SOL for the transaction fee and any required account rent.

### Priority fee too low

If the claim sits pending or expires, retry with a higher priority-fee setting.

### Claiming across many farms

Large batch claims can fail if too many positions or reward streams are included. Claim a few positions at a time.

## Verification

After harvest or collection:

- Check that the transaction status is **Success**.
- Confirm the reward or fee token balance increased in your wallet.
- Refresh Raydium's portfolio page and confirm pending amounts reset or decreased.
- For a zero-liquidity CLMM collection, confirm the position's liquidity did not decrease.
