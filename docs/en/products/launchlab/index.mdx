## What it is

LaunchLab is Raydium's token-launch program. A project initializes a token against a bonding curve and buyers trade during the `Fund` state. Reaching the funding threshold moves the launch to `Migrate`. The authorized migration wallet then creates a CPMM pool, after which the launch reaches `Trade` and normal pool trading can begin.

**Program ID:** see [reference/program-addresses](/docs/en/reference/program-addresses).
