## What it is

CPMM is Raydium's standard constant-product AMM. It is the successor to AMM v4. It supports Token-2022 mints, including transfer-fee tokens, and has an optional creator fee and an on-chain price oracle. A CPMM swap uses more compute and more accounts than an AMM v4 V2 swap; see [Overview](/docs/en/products/cpmm/overview#how-cpmm-differs-from-amm-v4).

**Program ID:** see [reference/program-addresses](/docs/en/reference/program-addresses).

**Token-2022:** supported for mints whose extensions are all on the CPMM allow-list: `TransferFeeConfig`, `MetadataPointer`, `TokenMetadata`, `InterestBearingConfig` and `ScaledUiAmount`. A mint with any other extension, such as `PermanentDelegate` or `DefaultAccountState`, needs an admin-created `SupportMintAssociated` record. See [Accounts](/docs/en/products/cpmm/accounts#vaults-and-token-2022) for the rule, and [algorithms/token-2022-transfer-fees](/docs/en/algorithms/token-2022-transfer-fees) for how transfer fees affect swap math.
